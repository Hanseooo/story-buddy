# Labelling rulebook — Objective 4

**Frozen by:** `PREREGISTRATION_OBJ4.md` §12, 2026-09-14 · **Applies to:** `/annotate` rounds 1 and 2,
and `/adjudicate`

This is the "pre-registered study rubric" the annotation screen refers to. It exists because there is
one rater and round 2 is the same person two weeks later: agreement with yourself is only as good as
the rules you are both following. **No rule here changes once the first non-pilot label exists.** A
case the rules do not cover gets the closest rule, and a line in your private notes for the write-up.
It never gets a new rule. The screen has no notes field and shows no pair ID, so keep the notes in a
separate file: the date and time, what you saw, and what you chose.

## The one principle

**Judge only what the two images show.** Image 1 is the character's reference. Image 2 is a book page.
You are never shown the story, and neither is the judge, so a change the story explains (she grew up,
she is in a costume) and a change the generator invented look the same to both of you. Never use what
you remember about a story, and never assume a page is right because it was drawn from that reference.

**Different needs a difference you can name.** The screen will not accept Different without at least
one drift reason. If you cannot point at a listed reason, the answer is Same.

## Step 1 — find the character

Compare only the character in the reference. Other characters on the page do not count.

| What you see | Label |
|---|---|
| The character is not on the page | **Different** + Character Absent |
| Several figures could be them | Compare the figure that matches best |
| Drawn twice | Compare the better match; a duplicate is not an identity failure |

## Step 2 — identity

**Same** when species, face, body colours and body features all match the reference.

**Different** when any of these differs. Tick every reason that applies:

| Reason | Counts | Does not count |
|---|---|---|
| Wrong Color | Skin, fur, hair, eye or body colour | Clothing colour (that is clothing) |
| Wrong Species | Animal type or core silhouette | Pose, angle |
| Wrong Body Feature | Countable or structural parts: eyes, ears, tail, horns, wings, limbs | A part hidden by the pose |
| Different Face | Face shape, eye style, facial markings of another individual | Expression, mouth open, looking away |

## Step 3 — things that are not identity on their own

Each of these alone is **Same**. Tick it as a reason only when the pair is already Different. The
screen will accept Different with only Wrong Clothing or Wrong Style ticked; that combination is
always a mistake under these rules, so check for it before you submit.

- **Clothing and accessories** changed, added or missing (Wrong Clothing/Accessories).
- **Art style** looks off (Wrong Style). References and pages share one style preset.
- **Lighting, shadow, weather, dirt, wetness.**

## Step 4 — the hard cases

| Case | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Older or younger** | Same if face, body colours and features still match. Different if they do not (grey hair is Wrong Color; a new face shape is Different Face). Being an adult is not a reason by itself. |
| **Costume or disguise** | Clothing alone is Same. If it hides the face, use the next row. |
| **Back view, hidden face, silhouette, very small** | Judge what is visible. Same when nothing visible contradicts the reference. Never mark Different only because the face cannot be seen. |
| **Transformation** (turned into an animal, a ghost) | No special case: apply Step 2. A changed species is Wrong Species. |
| **Partly cut off by the frame** | Judge what is visible, as above. |

## Step 5 — the two artifact checkboxes

Independent of Same or Different. Tick them on either answer.

- **Broken Anatomy:** merged, missing or duplicated body parts on the character as drawn on the page
  (image 2), not on the reference.
- **Text Visible:** any letters, numbers or writing anywhere on the page.

## Procedure

- Open the lightbox when a detail decides the label. Do not guess at thumbnail size.
- Round 2: same rules, cold, about two weeks after round 1. Do not re-read your notes first.
- Adjudication (round 3) applies these same rules to the pairs where your rounds disagreed.
