# Audit 3: research docs hub, research landing page, and the paper

Read-only. Date: 2026-11-21. Repo at `main` = `origin/main` (`git rev-list --count origin/main..main` → 0, and the reverse → 0).
Paper: the work-in-progress `paper.md` in the scratchpad, cited as `paper:<line>`.

## Bottom line

- **Every hub link works.** `pnpm exec vitest run "app/(research)/research/(reference)/docs/"` → `Test Files 2 passed, Tests 18 passed`. The repo is public (`gh repo view` → `"visibility":"PUBLIC"`), and the spot-checked targets exist on `origin/main`.
- **Several linked documents are out of date.** The "today" pipeline diagram, `group-guide.md` and `methodology.md` all predate the 2026-11-21 switch that turned teacher-held moderation and the magic pencil on by default. Two of them also predate the August move to 10 pages and 3 attempts.
- **The hub leaves out the files the paper's Methodology chapter needs most:** system architecture, instruments, ethics, ISO/IEC 25010, hardware. `hardware_and_hosting.md` is not just missing from the hub. A July commit cut it down to the 4-byte string `proc`, and ADR-026, ADR-027, `annotation-surface.md` and `TECH_STACK.md` still cite sections of it.
- **The paper and the repo disagree in about 15 places.** These include the school, the validator panel, the number of questionnaire items, the Likert anchors, the ISO characteristics, the page count (10–15 vs 10), the module count, the story counts (13 vs 12), who the user is, and the hosting region. The repo's own rule (`methodology.md:7`, `research_direction_and_goals.md:4-6`) makes the manuscript authoritative on scope, respondents and instruments. So where the paper is newer, the repo docs are the stale side. The owner has to settle each one; I have not picked a side.
- **The research landing page (`/research`) is the stalest surface.** It says 11 nodes against 14 in `graph.py`, makes a self-hostable claim the repo itself rejects, and gives no objectives, status or results summary that a groupmate could write the paper from.
