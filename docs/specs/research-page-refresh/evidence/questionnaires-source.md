## 1. Expert evaluation (Objective 3)

### Informed consent (terms)

- Study: "StoryBuddy: An AI-Powered Storyboarding and Picture Book Generation System to Support
  Teachers and Pre-Service Educators in Creating Digital Picture Books."
- Participation: review an original written story with the digital picture book generated from it,
  then complete a seven-question, open-ended questionnaire about the quality and presentation of
  the generated picture book.
- Voluntary. The evaluator may decline, or withdraw before submitting the completed questionnaire,
  without penalty, and may ask the researchers for clarification.
- Access to research data is limited to the researchers, their advisers and authorized evaluators.
  Materials are kept until the research has been completed and approved. Only selected outputs may
  appear in presentations or publications, under the study's data-handling arrangements.
- Choices: "I agree to participate" / "I do not agree to participate", with signature and date.

### Questionnaire

**Overview.** StoryBuddy is an AI-powered storyboarding and picture-book generation system designed
to transform Grade 5 and Grade 6 learners' written stories into illustrated digital picture books.
The system processes a submitted story, organizes it into storyboard scenes, generates corresponding
illustrations, and compiles the results into a digital picture book. This questionnaire gathers
expert evaluations about the generated picture book as a complete work. It also asks evaluators to
consider how well the generated book represents the original child-written story.

**I. Evaluator information.** Name (optional); professional position or role; field or area of
expertise (Visual Arts / Illustration, Education / Teaching, Others); years of experience; Story ID
(Story 1–5, Others); date.

**II. Instructions.**
- Review both the original child-written story and the corresponding generated picture book before
  answering.
- Evaluate the generated picture book as a complete work, considering its written content and visual
  presentation.
- Answer each question in your own words. Give explanations and examples, not a numerical rating or a
  yes/no response.
- If an aspect is outside your expertise or not applicable, say so and briefly explain.
- Materials: (1) the original child-written story and (2) the corresponding generated picture book.
- Complete a separate questionnaire for each book evaluated, with its Story ID.

**III. Questions.** The prompts under each question guide the response. They are not separate
questions.

1. **How appropriate and meaningful is the generated picture book for its intended readers?**
   Consider the relevance and suitability of its topic or theme, the clarity of its central idea or
   message, and whether its content is appropriate for Grade 5 and Grade 6 readers.
2. **How effectively does the written language communicate the story and engage its intended
   readers?** Consider clarity, vocabulary, sentence construction, grammar, dialogue, tone, mood, and
   the use of expressive or literary language where appropriate.
3. **How well are the characters and setting developed and represented throughout the picture
   book?** Consider whether characters are understandable and distinguishable, whether their actions
   fit the story, and whether the setting is clear and supports the events.
4. **How coherent and effective is the development of the plot from beginning to end?** Consider the
   sequence and connection of events, pacing, transitions, conflict or central situation,
   resolution, and whether important narrative details are missing, repeated, or confusing.
5. **How effectively do the illustrations communicate and support the written narrative?** Consider
   visual clarity and appeal, relevance to the events, representation of characters and setting, the
   relationship between text and images, and the way the illustrations contribute to mood or
   meaning.
6. **How faithfully does the generated picture book represent the original child-written story?**
   Consider whether the central idea, intended meaning, characters, key events, and important details
   are preserved, and whether any additions, omissions, or changes affect the child's intended
   narrative.
7. **Considering the generated picture book as a whole, how effective is its overall presentation and
   reading experience, and what improvements would you recommend?** Consider the organization of
   pages, readability, placement of text and illustrations, visual flow, reader engagement, overall
   strengths, and specific improvements that could enhance the picture book.

**Additional comments:** any further observations not covered above.

## 2. ISO/IEC 25010 questionnaire (Objective 5)

### Informed consent (terms)

- Same study title. Participation: use the StoryBuddy application and answer a questionnaire based
  on the ISO/IEC 25010 software quality model about the experience.
- **Mismatch (O-12):** the consent text says "a 25-item questionnaire" covering "five software
  quality characteristics: Functional Suitability, Usability, Reliability, Performance Efficiency,
  Compatibility, and Security". It lists six, and the questionnaire has 23 items. Section B is titled
  Interaction Capability, not Usability.
- Choices: agree / do not agree, with signature and date.

### Questionnaire — "Based on the ISO/IEC 25010:2023 Software Quality Model"

**Overview.** StoryBuddy is an AI-powered storyboarding and picture book generation system designed
to transform Grade 5 and 6 learners' written stories into digital picture books. It aims to support
teachers and pre-service educators in creating illustrated learning materials. The system processes
the submitted story, organizes it into storyboard scenes, generates corresponding illustrations, and
compiles the results into a digital picture book. The questionnaire evaluates the software quality of
the application: how well its features function, how users interact with the system, how reliably it
processes requests, how efficiently it performs generation tasks, and how securely it handles
submitted information.

**I. Evaluator information.** Name (optional); years of experience; professional position or role
(Teacher, IT Professional, IT Student (Graduating), Other); date. O-12: the role boxes do not list
education students.

**II. Instructions.** Rate each statement based on your experience using the application.
5 = Strongly Agree, 4 = Agree, 3 = Neutral, 2 = Disagree, 1 = Strongly Disagree.

**A. Functional Suitability** — whether StoryBuddy provides the functions needed to transform written
stories into digital picture books.
1. StoryBuddy provides the necessary functions to complete the digital picture-book generation
   process.
2. StoryBuddy allows users to submit written stories and provide the required information for
   processing.
3. StoryBuddy correctly organizes submitted stories into a sequence of storyboard scenes.
4. StoryBuddy generates the expected illustrations based on the information processed from each
   story scene.
5. StoryBuddy provides the necessary functions to compile and export the generated picture book.

**B. Interaction Capability** — how easy StoryBuddy is to understand, learn, and operate.
6. StoryBuddy's interface makes its available functions and features easy to recognize.
7. The instructions and information provided by the system make it easy to learn how to use its
   features.
8. The available controls and options allow users to operate the system effectively.
9. StoryBuddy provides appropriate feedback when users enter incomplete or invalid information.

**C. Reliability** — StoryBuddy's ability to perform its functions correctly and consistently under
normal operating conditions.
10. StoryBuddy performs its intended functions without unexpected errors during normal use.
11. StoryBuddy remains accessible and operational when needed under its intended operating
    conditions.
12. StoryBuddy handles processing interruptions or operation failures without causing unnecessary
    disruption to other functions.
13. StoryBuddy provides appropriate options to retry or recover an operation after an unexpected
    failure.
14. StoryBuddy preserves submitted story information throughout the picture-book generation process.

**D. Performance Efficiency** — response time, picture book generation speed, and responsiveness
during use.
15. StoryBuddy provides timely updates on the progress of picture-book generation.
16. StoryBuddy completes picture-book generation within an acceptable amount of time under normal
    operating conditions.
17. StoryBuddy maintains acceptable performance when processing stories within its expected workload
    and operating limits.

**E. Security** — how StoryBuddy protects submitted stories and associated information and
communicates its data-handling practices.
18. StoryBuddy protects submitted stories and associated information from unauthorized disclosure.
19. StoryBuddy protects stored story information against unauthorized modification.
20. StoryBuddy provides appropriate means to identify or verify authorized users when accessing
    protected features.
21. StoryBuddy provides safeguards against unauthorized attempts to access or compromise its functions
    and information.

**F. Compatibility** — StoryBuddy's ability to operate effectively within its intended computing
environment and exchange information with connected systems or services.
22. StoryBuddy operates properly within its supported browsers and computing environments.
23. StoryBuddy successfully exchanges the required information with its integrated AI services.
